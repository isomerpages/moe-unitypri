<h2>Singapore Youth Festival 2026</h2>
<p>Our Chinese Dance team delivered a captivating performance at the Singapore
Youth Festival (SYF) Arts Presentation and was awarded a Certificate of
Accomplishment. This accolade is testimony to the girls' dedication and
hard work over months of intensive preparation. Our dancers demonstrated
grit, passion, and determination as they balanced the rigours of their
academic work with those of their SYF rehearsals. We are proud of their
strong team spirit and the inspiring resilience they showcased throughout
this rewarding journey!</p>

<p>The Modern Dance CCA attained the Distinction award at the recent Singapore
Youth Festival (SYF) Arts Presentation. This achievement reflects the dancers’
dedication, passion, and commitment throughout their journey.</p>
<p>Our students demonstrated commendable resilience and responsibility as
they balanced academic demands with intensive training. They supported
one another, persevered through challenges and consistently strived for
improvement, culminating in a confident and polished performance on stage.</p>
<p>We extend our heartfelt appreciation to our parents for their unwavering
encouragement and support. We are also grateful to our parent volunteers
for their invaluable help, especially in assisting with the students’ make-up
and hairstyling on the day of the performance.</p>
<p>This achievement is a testament to the collective effort of our students,
parents and supporters. We look forward to continuing this journey of growth
and excellence together.</p>
