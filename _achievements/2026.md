<h4>Angklung/Kulintang Ensemble</h4>
<p>Over four months, our Angklung Ensemble worked tirelessly to prepare for
their performance at the SYF Arts Presentation 2026, learning and mastering
two challenging musical scores.</p>
<p>Under the guidance of a new instructor, the students adapted well and
remained committed and resilient throughout the journey, putting in consistent
effort during every practice session. Many also demonstrated admirable
dedication by attending additional rehearsals arranged at short notice
to polish their performance and improve their coordination as an ensemble.</p>
<p>Their hard work paid off as they were awarded Distinction at the presentation,
making the experience even more meaningful and rewarding for the entire
team.</p>

<h4>Football</h4>
<p>Unity’s Senior Football Team competed in the National School Games and
achieved an impressive 3rd place overall in League 3. Throughout the fiercely
contested tournament, the boys demonstrated great resilience.</p>
<p>Apart from one drawn match, the team emerged victorious in all their other
games during the group and pool stages, securing a well-deserved place
in the semi-finals. Although they were defeated in the semi-final match,
the team bounced back strongly in the 3rd/4th placing match, clinching
a convincing victory to finish the competition in 3rd place.</p>
<p>Kudos to the team for displaying exceptional grit, determination and sportsmanship
throughout the competition.</p>

<h4>Bangsawan Cup (Malay Opera Theatre Competition)</h4>
<p>On 20 May 2026, our talented P2 to P4 students took centre stage at the
Singapore Bangsawan Cup held at the Malay Heritage Centre with their performance
of <em>Tun Jana Katib Membelah Pokok</em>. This traditional Malay opera
competition provided a meaningful platform for students to showcase their
creativity, teamwork and appreciation for the Malay language through drama
and performance.</p>
<p>In the weeks leading to the competition, the students trained diligently
and demonstrated great commitment and enthusiasm. Their hard work and perseverance
shone through during the full-dress rehearsals, as they performed confidently
in costume and used props on stage to great effect.</p>
<p>On the day of the competition, the Malay Language teachers and Parent
Volunteers came together to cheer on the students. Our young performers
delivered a confident and outstanding performance before the panel of judges
and members of the audience.</p>
<p>We are proud to share that Unity Primary School emerged as Champion for
the second consecutive year and clinched the following special awards:</p>

</li>

</li>
