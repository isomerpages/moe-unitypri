<h3><strong>Our School Crest</strong></h3>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width:180px;height:180px;margin-left:15px;" height="auto" width="100%" src="/images/Our%20Unity%20Story/School%20Logo.png">
</div>
<p>The&nbsp;<strong>3 ribbons</strong>&nbsp;symbolize an “<strong>U</strong>pright”,
“<strong>U</strong>ndaunted” &amp; “<strong>U</strong>nited” Spirit &amp;
they carry&nbsp;<strong>3 messages</strong>:</p>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>To nurture confident &amp; all-rounded individuals.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To develop concerned &amp; active citizens with good character &amp; instill
in them sound moral values.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To strengthen the school-home-community partnership.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<p>The&nbsp;<strong>3 dots</strong>&nbsp;depict the&nbsp;<strong>3 initiatives</strong>,
namely, fostering</p>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Joy of Learning</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Creative &amp; Critical Thinking</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Teamwork</p>
</li>
</ol>
<p>The 3 colours represent:</p>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Blue signifies Respect &amp; Harmony.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Red signifies Resilience &amp; Care.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>White signifies Responsibility &amp; Integrity</p>
</li>
</ol>
